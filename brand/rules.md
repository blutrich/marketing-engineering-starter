# Review rules — {{BRAND_NAME}}

These are starter rules. Adapt them to your brand; preserve evidence and approval boundaries. Review findings must cite a rule ID and the exact draft wording.

1. **R1 — Supported claims.** Every capability, number, price, availability statement and customer result has evidence in the campaign source register. Omit unsupported claims or record them as questions.
2. **R2 — Availability stays explicit.** Available, beta, limited, planned and unknown are different. Preserve qualifiers; a roadmap item is not a released feature.
3. **R3 — Recognizable voice.** Apply [voice-guide.md](voice-guide.md), including its vocabulary and examples. Do not infer brand voice from a company name.
4. **R4 — One reader and one message.** Address the brief's audience and its single useful point. Explain what the reader can do without implying unsupported outcomes.
5. **R5 — A complete action.** Match the CTA and destination to the approved brief. Record a missing or untested destination; do not invent a working link.
6. **R6 — Honest evidence.** No invented testimonials, usage data, certifications, competitive comparisons or performance results. Example data must remain labeled fictional.
7. **R7 — Review is distinct from approval.** Facts, voice and completeness receive separate passes against the same draft and input versions. Any change to the draft or inputs invalidates all three reviews and any prior approval. Repeat all three; human approval is recorded separately.
8. **R8 — Approval is distinct from publication.** A review pass is not permission to publish. Record external action only after it actually happens, with evidence.

## Resolution

Any failed rule blocks a recommendation to approve. Reviewers state `pass`, `needs_changes` or `blocked`, with evidence; a voice pass also requires at least 9/10, and that score never overrides a failed rule. Unresolved evidence or a missing decision goes to the named owner.
