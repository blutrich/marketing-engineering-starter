# About this starter

Prepared for Ofer Blutrich's talk, "Building Harnesses for Agents That Empower the Marketing Team," at the AImpowered Product Managers meetup hosted by evoke, November 2, 2026.

This is an independent educational starter. Its workflow was inspired by the architectural ideas in the [Base44 Marketing plugin](https://github.com/base44-dev/marketing): sources before writing, specialist review, explicit workflow state and reusable lessons. It is not the Base44 Marketing plugin, a supported Base44 product, or an official distribution of that plugin.

The code, instructions, templates and fictional examples in this starter were written for this package. No plugin implementation, company brand rules, company learning logs, internal sources, design assets or connector configuration were copied into it. The MIT license in this folder applies to this newly written package; it does not grant rights to third-party material a participant may add later.

Fieldnote and its feature information are fictional teaching material. Example drafts and review results are demonstration fixtures. The replay does not call an AI model, authenticate reviewers, grant human approval or publish anything. Use your own authorized sources and brand configuration for a real exercise.

This starter deliberately uses a human approval step and local-only operations. These are teaching choices, not a description of every workflow in the production plugin.
