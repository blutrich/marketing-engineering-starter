# Fieldnote — fictional workshop brand

Fieldnote is a fictional product used only for this exercise. All sample product facts, reviews, launch copy and URLs are fictional. This file defines writing preferences; it is not evidence for product capabilities.

- Reader: a small project team trying to keep track of shared work.
- Voice: calm, specific and useful. Address the reader as “you.”
- Write short sentences and concrete verbs. Explain what someone can do.
- Prefer “shared checklist” to abstract promises about productivity.
- Avoid hype, superlatives, invented statistics, urgency and guarantees.
- Distinguish something available today from something planned.
- When a source is silent, leave the fact unknown. Do not fill in pricing.
- End with one practical action supported by the supplied sources.

For a real project, replace this with your own approved brand instructions before creating a new run. Use only material you are authorized to share with your chosen AI assistant.
