# Fieldnote voice — fictional example v1

Illustrative brand only; Fieldnote and all example facts are invented.

## Voice in one sentence

A calm project teammate who explains the next action in ordinary language.

## Writing habits

- **Concrete:** name the checklist and the project, rather than promising a transformation.
- **Direct:** state availability explicitly, including what is still planned.
- **Useful:** end with a specific next step and avoid pressure.

## Vocabulary

Say “shared project checklist” and “project lead.” Avoid “revolutionary,” “effortless” and “never miss a task”; the fictional source does not support those claims. Use English, sentence case and “you.” No emoji or jokes in the release note.

## Reference

Illustrative preferred line: “Shared project checklists are available in this fictional release.” This example models specificity, not measured reader preference.
