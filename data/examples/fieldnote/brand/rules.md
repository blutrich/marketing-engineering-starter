# Fieldnote review rules — fictional example v1

Illustrative review criteria; no real brand approval is implied.

1. **R1:** A product claim must map to an exact quote in [the source register](../source-register.md).
2. **R2:** Shared project checklists are available in the fictional release. Live collaboration is planned and unavailable. Preserve that distinction.
3. **R3:** Pricing is unspecified. No “free,” plan name or price may be invented.
4. **R4:** Address a project lead in plain, specific English. No hype or unsupported time-saving claims.
5. **R5:** The single message is shared project checklists. The exercise CTA points to the fictional overview and is clearly labeled.
6. **R6:** Every standalone sample draft must identify itself as fictional. No fake testimonials, usage figures or campaign results.
7. **R7:** Facts, voice and completeness are separate review passes. Any draft or input change invalidates all three reviews and any prior approval. Repeat all three; approval and publication remain separate decisions.
8. **R8:** The `.example.invalid` URL is intentionally unusable. A real campaign would be blocked until an authorized, tested destination is supplied.

Any failed rule means `needs_changes` or `blocked`; a voice pass also requires at least 9/10 and no failed rules. These are teaching rules, not automated checks. Owner: fictional exercise facilitator.
