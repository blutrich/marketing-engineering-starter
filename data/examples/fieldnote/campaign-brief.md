# Campaign brief — Fieldnote shared checklists

- Brief version: B1.

## Purpose

Show a project lead what the invented shared checklist release includes, while preserving the distinction between available and planned capabilities.

- **Audience:** project leads coordinating a small team's work; an illustrative hypothesis, not research.
- **One message:** shared project checklists are available in the fictional release.
- **Channel / format:** one text feature announcement, suitable for a workshop handout.
- **CTA:** Read the fictional overview.
- **Destination:** `https://fieldnote.example.invalid/checklists`, owned by no real operator; deliberately non-working.
- **Deliverables:** one sample announcement, claim map and review records.
- **Excluded:** real publication, pricing offers, competitive claims and generated visuals.

## Unknowns and constraints

Pricing, rollout scope, release date and real destinations are unknown. The facilitator owns questions for this exercise. A real campaign owner would have to supply authorized evidence and a working destination. Do not imply live editing, quantified savings or customer adoption.

## Success and decision

The exercise succeeds when a participant identifies the unsupported availability claim, replaces it with the supported wording and explains why review does not equal publication. Evaluate using the review record; no participant result has been measured and there is no campaign-performance baseline.
